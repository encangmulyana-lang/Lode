Questions, answered

FAQ

Is the first mint really free?

Yes, if your wallet holds a Binance Agent ID (.ai). Your first Lode is free. After that, each Lode costs 0.0006 BNB, plus gas.

How do I earn $MINE?

Hold a Lode. It mines 100 $MINE × its power every day. Claim it on-chain on the Mine page — your yield is never stored in a browser.

What is inside my rock?

A mineral with a rarity and a mining power: Iron (55%, power 1), Quartz (22%, power 2), Copper (13%, power 3), Gold (7%, power 5), or Diamond (3%, power 10). The mineral is fixed on-chain at mint.

Can I buy or sell a Lode?

Yes — trading is live on the Marketplace page. List your rock at a price you choose, or buy one from another holder. The marketplace takes a 5% fee on each sale.
